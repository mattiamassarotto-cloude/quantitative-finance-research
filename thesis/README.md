# Bachelor's Thesis

## Title

**From the Efficient Market Hypothesis to the Challenges of Modern Finance: Algorithmic Trading, Quantitative Models and Financial Crises**

## Abstract

This bachelor's thesis investigates the limitations of the Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH) during periods of financial stress and market instability.

The experimental section includes the implementation of several quantitative finance models developed in Python:

- GARCH volatility modelling
- DCC-GARCH dynamic correlations
- CAPM
- Markowitz portfolio optimization
- Financial return distribution analysis

The objective is to evaluate the behaviour of classical financial models under different market conditions and highlight the impact of financial crises on their assumptions.
